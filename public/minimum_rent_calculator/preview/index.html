How this works

Add up what the whole building costs each month. The rented units cover their share: in a 2-flat where you live in one unit, the rented unit covers half; in a 3-flat where you live in one, the two rented units cover two thirds between them. The unit you live in carries the rest, as your own housing cost. Savings for empty months between tenants and unpaid rent are added on top.

Simple mode uses typical amounts for repairs, big replacements, your pay, savings and vacancies. Advanced mode lets you set each one.
